About course

IELTS prepares you for international English exams with focused practice in speaking, listening, reading, and writing. Learn effective strategies to achieve your target score.

The directions of study:

Listening

about

Focused practice with real exam formats to improve accuracy, note-taking, and understanding of different accents.

Reading

about

Effective strategies for skimming, scanning and answering tricky question types under time pressure.

Writing

about

Structured approach to Task 1 and Task 2, with personal feedback to improve coherence, grammar and vocabulary.

Speaking

about

Mock interviews and personal feedback to build fluency, confidence, and hit your target band score.
