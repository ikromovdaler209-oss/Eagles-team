Pre IELTS

Intensive IELTS preparation & strong vocabulary fundaments

About course

Grammar, vocabulary and everyday conversation, structured by level from A1 to C1.

The directions of study:

Listening skills

Accents & Speed

Build a strong foundation in listening comprehension. Practice understanding different accents, speeds, and contexts to prepare for the IELTS format.

Reading Skils

Key Strategies

Develop reading strategies for academic and general texts. Learn to identify key information and manage time effectively before the full IELTS course.

Basic Writing

Solid Structure

Learn the fundaments of structured writing - sentence building, paragraphing, and clarity. A strong base before tackling IELTS Writing tasks.

Speaking Foundations

Build Confidence

Build confidence and fluency in spoken English. Practice everyday and exam-style questions to get comfortable before the IELTS Speaking module.
