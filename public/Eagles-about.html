Who're We?

Eagles-team is a training centre that specializes in preparing students for IELTS, pre-IELTS, CEFR exams, and building strong English grammar skills. We unite experienced teachers with a practice-oriented approach, so that each student does not just study theory, but immediately applies the knowledge in practice. The training format is selected individually, and the progress of each student is under constant control of our teachers.
