About course

CEFR Prep helps you determine and improve your English level according to the international CEFR framework (A1–C2). Build confidence, fluency, and accuracy across all key language skills.

The directions of study:

Level Assessment

Find Your Level

Comprehensive testing to accurately determine your current CEFR level, from A1 to C2.

Grammar & Vocabilary

Strengthen Basics

Structured lessons to strengthen grammar accuracy and expand vocabulary for your target level.

Listening & Reading

Authentic Practice

Practice with authentic materials to improve comprehension skills required for CEFR certification.

Speaking & Writing

Clear Expression

Develop clear, confident expression in both spoken and written English to meet CEFR standards.
